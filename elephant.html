ELEPHANT

modular solar-canopy EV charging station & urban energy kiosk

Rapid electric vehicle adoption demands charging infrastructure that integrates effortlessly into contemporary urban architecture rather than cluttering streets with utilitarian cabinets.

Elephant reimagines high-output EV charging as a modular architectural canopy system. Combining sculptural load-bearing columns, overhead photovoltaic arrays, and intuitive dual-bay kiosk terminals, the station delivers autonomous solar buffer storage and frictionless cable ergonomics.

Background

As electric vehicle fleets expand globally, public charging stations are transitioning from hidden highway rest stops to high-visibility civic landmarks. Infrastructure must balance high-power solar buffering, weather protection, and intuitive driver interaction from the moment of arrival.

Grid Peak Demand

High-output fast chargers create sudden power spikes on municipal distribution grids.

  • Organic branching solar canopy
  • Perforated battery-buffer base
  • Modular multi-bay scalability

User & Cable Friction

Heavy charging cables dragging on wet pavement degrade user comfort and hardware longevity.

  • Cantilevered "trunk" overhead holster
  • 360° glowing green status halo
  • Zero-drag suspended nozzle reach

Architectural Presence

Conventional sheet-metal charging boxes lack visual cohesion in modern urban environments.

  • Monolithic brushed-metal silhouette
  • Recessed cyan ambient light channel
  • Compact civic bay footprint

Charging Infrastructure Challenges

The primary design challenge lies in uniting solar energy harvesting, overhead cable ergonomics, and circular-economy incentives into a single cohesive architectural landmark.

How might we design a biomorphic, solar-assisted EV charging station that elevates urban streetscapes while rewarding sustainable driver habits?

The Idea

Inspired directly by the powerful yet graceful silhouette of an elephant's head and trunk, Elephant cantilevers the charging connector from an overhead arched arm while housing interactive telemetry and a reverse-vending bottle recycling intake in its main column.

FINAL DESIGN

Circular Recycling & Package

Beneath the high-contrast charging telemetry display sits an illuminated circular intake port. While waiting for their vehicle to charge, drivers can insert used PET plastic bottles into the kiosk to receive instant charging fee discounts—turning idle charging time into tangible environmental action.
