Interior Design

Transformative commercial interiors and collaborative design studios shaped by brand heritage, fluid zoning, and bespoke architectural framing. Every space is conceived to balance raw industrial shells with precision-crafted luxury.

01 / Volume

Implied Boundaries

Stripping back rigid partitions to create open-plan ecosystems using tension cables and floor transitions to define private zones without blocking natural light or sightlines.

02 / Materiality

Raw & Refined Surfaces

Contrasting untreated, monolithic textures like raw stone and micro-cement with the warmth of oak wood and precision-machined dark metal.

03 / Illumination

Choreographed Light

Maximizing natural daylight through open sightlines, paired with integrated LED ceiling strips and targeted task lighting to create an energetic, focused workspace.

04 / Custom Fixtures

The Weaver's Arch

Abstracting traditional Sassanid geometries into modern, semi-transparent wireframe meeting pods and suspended, gravity-defying collaborative desks.

Chapter 01  ·  Dorsa Design Studio

Adaptive Reuse & Brand Architecture

Designed as a fluid collaborative ecosystem, this 140-square-meter adaptive reuse project transforms a rigid administrative unit into a transparent, open-plan workspace. By utilizing vertical tension cables to "weave" the space, it unites the Design and Marketing teams while physically embodying the Dorsa brand’s luxurious, crafted identity.
