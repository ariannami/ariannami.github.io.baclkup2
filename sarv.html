SARV

architectural luminaire & ambient lighting product

Inspired by the monolithic vertical posture of the Persian cypress tree (Sarv), this lighting product explores how a pure architectural silhouette can shape warm, glare-free ambient illumination in contemporary interiors.

By surrounding a central linear LED diffuser tube with 18 radial burl-wood and brushed-aluminum louver fins seated on a fluted pedestal and weighted wood plinth, Sarv transitions effortlessly between a serene sculptural column by day and an atmospheric light source at night.

Background

Modern living and workspace interiors often suffer from harsh directional downlighting or visually cluttered fixture hardware. The objective was to craft a minimal floor luminaire that delivers soft vertical wall-washing and warm organic tactility in both daylight and evening settings.

Glare Elimination

Exposed LED diodes create visual fatigue when viewed directly in living spaces.

  • 18 radial optical louver fins
  • Rhythmic vertical wall-wash
  • Warm 2700K core diffusion

Material Warmth

Purely metallic fixtures can feel cold and clinical when unlit during the day.

  • Natural burl-wood inner faces
  • Brushed aluminum outer edges
  • Internal glowing warm reflections

Structural Stability

Slender vertical floor lamps require a low center of gravity without bulky footprints.

  • Fluted conical lower pedestal
  • Weighted timber & steel plinth
  • Braided textile cord routing

Ambient Lighting Challenges

Balancing optical softness, natural timber warmth, and sculptural presence within a slender footprint guided every stage of the design process.

How might we translate the timeless silhouette of the Persian cypress into a precision-crafted luminaire that shapes atmosphere with effortless warmth?

The Idea

Rooted in the iconic form of the Persian cypress (Sarv), the luminaire arranges 18 tapered composite fins radially around a frosted tubular light core. Light grazes the rich burl-wood inner surfaces of each fin before projecting outward in soft architectural ribs.

02

FINAL DESIGN

Package & Assembly

Internal components assemble coaxially around the central tubular diffuser and brushed steel column. Precision-milled slots in the internal retention ring and top cap lock all 18 radial wood-and-aluminum fins into alignment above the fluted lower pedestal.

Atmospheric Throw & Form

From intimate evening corners where radial louvers paint warm light stripes across the wall to sculptural studio elevations, Sarv anchors the room as functional art.
